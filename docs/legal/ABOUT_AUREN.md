# English

## 1. Product

Auren is Personal Health Intelligence with the primary promise: Data → Pattern → Meaning → One useful decision.

Its primary tagline is “Your body, understood.”

## 2. Studio

Auren is a Benedict Interactive product. Benedict Interactive is the studio identity used for the product, design, engineering and stewardship of Auren.

Nothing in this screen should be read as a representation that Benedict Interactive has a particular corporate or registered legal form unless that status is separately and expressly published.

## 3. Product philosophy

Auren is built around trust before intelligence, personal baseline before judgment, evidence before claims, progressive disclosure, local-first privacy and no fake precision.

Auren is intentionally not a guilt-streak product, calorie diary, medical diagnosis app or AI chatbot where AI is the hero.

## 4. Health boundaries

Auren provides wellbeing context, not medical care. It distinguishes what you observed, what was calculated and what was inferred, and it should expose important limitations instead of pretending certainty.

## 5. Privacy direction

The current Auren generation requires no account and stores ordinary profile/check-in history locally on the device/browser. Cloud features are not a hidden dependency and should be added only when clear user value and an appropriate trust/legal model exist.

## 6. Intellectual property

Auren’s proprietary status protects only rights that applicable law recognises. General health knowledge, public-domain information, ideas, methods and third-party rights remain outside Auren’s exclusive ownership.

## 7. Legal framework

The Legal Center contains the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Copyright & Intellectual Property Notice and Third-Party Notices. The app Build number is separate from the Legal Version.

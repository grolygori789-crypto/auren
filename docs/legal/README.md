## Legal Version vs Build

Legal Version is independent from the runtime Build number.

- Material contractual/privacy changes: increment Legal Version and review renewed acknowledgement.
- Runtime changes with no legal effect: increment Build only.
- Editorial corrections with no material change to rights may retain Legal Version after review.

## Important

The in-app Legal Center is generated from the same EN/TH semantic source as these documents.

No legal template can guarantee enforceability in every jurisdiction. Before a major paid/store launch or remote health-data processing, qualified technology/privacy/IP counsel should review the legal identity of the operator/rights holder, official private contact channel, health claims, governing-law language, liability terms and data flows.
